## What is MarginCraft?

Imagine you have a lemonade stand, but instead of lemonade you make things with a 3D printer or a laser. MarginCraft is a helper that tells you how much to charge so you don't lose money.

## Why is that hard?

When you sell something, it's easy to only count the plastic or wood. But lots of other things cost money too:

- 🖨️ **Your machine gets tired.** One day you'll need a new one, so every job should save a little for it.
- ⚡ **Electricity** isn't free.
- 🙋 **Your time** is worth money. Sanding, packing and talking to customers count!
- 🏠 **Rent and bills** need paying even when the machine is resting.
- 🧾 **Taxes** take a slice too.

MarginCraft adds all of these up for you.

## Then what?

You choose how much extra you want to keep (that's your **profit**), and MarginCraft tells you the price. It can also look at what other people charge for the same thing, so your price isn't silly-high or silly-low.

## Is it safe?

Yes. Everything stays on your own computer, in your browser. Nobody else sees your numbers.

## Extra tricks

- ✉️ It can write a nice offer letter for your customer.
- 📒 It can keep a diary of your prices in a Google spreadsheet.
- 🎒 You can save different setups, like "garage" and "real workshop", and switch with one click.
